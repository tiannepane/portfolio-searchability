Human Fallback Triggers

Not every insight collapses cleanly into a theme. When the clustering surfaces something rare but high-stakes, or something that flatly contradicts the pattern it's building, it doesn't force a fit — it flags it for a person to look at instead.

P7 — "Nearly switched vendors over a 6-week ticket" — got flagged this way: too significant to fold into an existing theme on the strength of one mention, and worth a human read before it's dismissed as noise.
